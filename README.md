A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. Lord Ashkar — breaker of the Nine Banners at Duskfield, drinker of a river, keeper of a king's skull as a paperweight for tax ledgers — receives yet another hero on yet another Tuesday, and finds he would rather negotiate his calendar than fight. The tiredness lives in the pauses, the flat observations and the small deflections, never in a plain statement of weariness. `main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` and validates the gate (UTF-8, 1–600 characters, about 100 words).
